# Team Work Log – W05

**Team:** D · **Project:** NewEra POS · **Recorded by:** Avni Oflaz
**Course topic:** Software process models

## Summary
- The teacher gave a required structure for the weekly progress presentations, limited to 10 minutes in total:
  1. Technical progress (system development).
  2. Experimental progress (deliverables).
  3. Project progress (team collaboration and management), limited to 2 minutes.
- The team prepared the W05 report slides following this structure.
- The team compared four process models: waterfall, prototyping, spiral/evolutionary and iterative-incremental.
- A Gantt schedule was added to the repository.

## Findings
- Our requirements are mostly known from the case, but details such as rental rules and external-system behaviour still need confirming. An incremental approach lets us deliver and review parts early.
- The course milestones match an incremental plan: SRS in W7, design in W11 and launch in W16.

## Decisions
- **Process model:** iterative-incremental. The project runs in three increments plus a release:
  - Increment 1: Requirements.
  - Increment 2: Design.
  - Increment 3: Construction and testing.
- Each team member owns part of the SRS.
- Each document is reviewed by another member through GitHub Issues.
- Roles were assigned for each member.

- Start Increment 1 (Requirements) in W06 with the SRS skeleton, use case model, stakeholders, conventions, external interfaces and glossary.
